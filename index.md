## How the Program Works

1. **Pitch an Asset:** Submit your idea using our structured YAML application template in GitHub Discussions.
2. **Community Upvoting:** The community votes on submissions to signal real demand and utility.
3. **Selection & Build:** Accepted candidates complete, document, and pass technical reviews prior to cohort launch.
4. **180-Day Residency:** Builders act as lead maintainers and evangelists for 6 months under the Apache 2.0 open-source license.
